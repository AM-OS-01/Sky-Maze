Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Information stored by Sky Maze

Sky Maze stores your travel information, traveler profiles, unfinished drafts, Travel Notes, attached photos and PDF confirmations, and app settings locally on your device. Notes and confirmations remain with their bookings when a trip moves to Previous Trips. A separate Sky Maze account is not required. Sky Maze does not sell your information or use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Local storage does not mean every feature works only on your device. When you submit files for scanning, their contents are processed by Sky Maze's backend and OpenAI. Device verification, subscriptions, location searches, and sharing also involve the services described below. Saved trips are not automatically uploaded to a Sky Maze cloud trip library.

Scanning screenshots, photos, and PDFs

When you submit selected files for scanning, Sky Maze prepares images on your device and sends them over HTTPS to its backend, hosted on Railway, which passes them to the OpenAI API to extract travel information. PDF pages are rendered as images for scanning; the original PDF and its file name are not sent through this scanning request. Image preparation removes embedded image metadata, but it does not redact information visible on the page.

The service can therefore receive any information visible in the files you submit, including names, contact information, travel dates, locations, reservation references, and information about other travelers. Only submit files you have permission to use, and remove unnecessary sensitive information before scanning. Selecting files alone does not start a scan. Attaching a file directly to a saved booking's Confirmations does not, by itself, submit it for AI scanning.

Extracted information is returned to the app for your review and correction before you save your trip. The app uses the returned source references to attach the selected confirmations to the corresponding bookings. A document covering multiple bookings may be attached to more than one booking. Original PDFs are retained locally as complete documents, not cropped to only the matching booking. Automated extraction and matching can be wrong, so review both the travel information and attached files.

The current backend processes scan images and extracted results in memory and does not write that content to its application database or application logs. Local drafts, prepared images, and original confirmation files may remain on your device while a scan or review is unfinished so you can recover your work. Saving or discarding the relevant draft cleans up its staging files; an interruption or failed cleanup may leave recovery data until the app next completes cleanup.

Sky Maze sends scan requests with OpenAI response storage disabled. This does not guarantee zero retention by OpenAI. Under OpenAI's published API policy, API data is not used for model training by default unless the API customer opts in; abuse-monitoring content may be retained for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Other temporary processing storage may apply. See OpenAI's API data controls. Deleting a local trip does not erase provider records already retained under those policies.

Device verification and service records

Sky Maze uses Apple's App Attest to help verify app installations and protect the scanning service from unauthorized use. Verification may run when the app opens, before you submit any files. It does not send screenshots or trip contents. The backend stores an app-installation key identifier, public verification key, attestation receipt, creation time, and anti-replay counter. These are security identifiers, not advertising identifiers.

To enforce scan limits and avoid duplicate requests, the backend stores request identifiers, a fingerprint of submitted image content, the request day, and a pseudonymous installation identifier. Older daily scan-limit records are removed when a new reservation for scanning is processed on a later day. Verification challenges expire after five minutes and are cleaned up after ten minutes when another challenge is created. Installation registration records currently have no automatic expiration.

Application error logs can include a request identifier but not uploaded images or extracted results. Hosting and network providers may also process connection information, such as IP addresses and request timing, to deliver and secure the service. Their operational records and backups have separate retention practices. Deleting the app or a trip does not automatically remove server-side security records. Contact us about those records or other privacy requests.

Backups and deletion

You can edit saved travel details and Travel Notes and delete individual attachments in the app. Remove Trip moves the active trip to Previous Trips; it does not delete the saved information. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips removes that trip and its associated Travel Notes, note photos, and confirmations from the app's local storage. Removing an imported attachment does not delete the original from Photos or Files.

Copies waiting to be imported from the share menu are stored separately. If an import cannot be completed, those copies may remain on your device even if you delete the intended trip. See Sharing into Sky Maze below. Deleting content in Sky Maze also does not delete copies you previously exported to another app or recipient.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting a trip in Sky Maze does not remove copies from existing backups. Sky Maze cannot access or remotely delete information stored on your device or in your backups.

Note photos

When you tap the camera in a Travel Note, Sky Maze requests camera access so you can take a photo for that note. You can also select an existing photo using Apple's photo picker; Sky Maze receives only the photos you select, without requesting access to your entire photo library. Photos saved to a note are stored locally as resized copies without the original photo's embedded metadata. Copies waiting to be imported from another app may still contain that metadata, as explained below. Captured photos are not automatically saved to your photo library or uploaded by Sky Maze. Importing or deleting a note attachment does not change the original photo in your library. Previously attached photos remain with their notes. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips also removes its note attachments from the app.

Confirmations

You can attach selected photos and PDF files to a booking using Apple's Photos and Files pickers. Sky Maze receives the items you choose, not unrestricted access to your photo library or files. If you choose a file stored with iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may download it to your device under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sky Maze saves local copies of these attachments. Photos saved to a booking are resized and re-encoded without the original photo's embedded metadata; copies waiting to be imported from another app may still contain that metadata. PDFs are copied in their original form and may retain embedded metadata and personal information. Sky Maze also stores thumbnails and, for PDFs, the file name and page count. Files submitted through the scan flow are processed as described above. Sky Maze does not access your email account or automatically update confirmations when a booking changes.

Attachments stay with the booking in Previous Trips until you delete the attachment or the saved trip. The PDF viewer may offer Apple's sharing controls; using them sends the selected document to the app or destination you choose. Review the document before sharing it.

Sharing into Sky Maze

When you choose Sky Maze in another app's share menu, Sky Maze receives the supported items that app passes with your action, such as selected PDFs, photos, or note text. You choose a travel event in your active trip as the destination. An active trip is required; Sky Maze does not offer a separate save-for-later collection.

Shared PDFs and photos can be saved as confirmations. Shared note text, including text sent as a copy from Apple Notes, is appended to the selected event's Travel Note, along with supported note photos. Text is imported as plain text, so formatting may differ from the original. This is a copy, not a connection to your Notes library or email account. Later changes in either app do not automatically update the other copy.

The app and its share extension use a shared storage area on your device. It holds a summary of available travel events so you can choose a destination, along with incoming items and the identifiers needed to attach them to the correct event. This does not give the source app unrestricted access to your saved trips.

Incoming copies are queued locally until Sky Maze saves them to the selected event. The queued copy is removed after a successful import. If an import fails or its destination is no longer available, the queued copy may remain on your device for recovery; there is no automatic expiration. These incoming copies can contain original photo or PDF metadata. They are not uploaded to a Sky Maze server.

Sharing a Travel Note

The share button inside a Travel Note opens Apple's share sheet with that note's text and attached note photos. It does not include the booking's separate confirmation files. Sky Maze prepares the content locally and removes its temporary export files when sharing finishes or is dismissed. You choose the receiving app or destination, such as Apple Notes, which may store or sync the received copy according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Sharing a note does not create ongoing synchronization. Editing or deleting the note in Sky Maze does not change copies already shared elsewhere. Review the text and photos before sharing, especially if they contain booking codes or other personal information.

Calendar

When you choose Add to Calendar, Sky Maze requests full calendar access through Apple's permission prompt so it can add your trip's events and find and update the calendar events it previously created. It stores the linked event identifiers and synchronization details locally on your device. It does not use unrelated calendar events for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Calendar events can include travel dates and times, routes, locations, company and service details, terminal, gate, seat, and booking confirmation codes when provided. Your Travel Note text, note photos, and attached confirmation files are not added to Calendar. Changes to travel details can update previously linked calendar events.

Events are saved to your default writable calendar and may sync through the account associated with that calendar, such as iCloud or another provider. That provider's privacy policy applies. Moving a trip to Previous Trips or deleting it from Sky Maze does not remove copies already saved to Calendar. You can manage those events in Calendar and change Sky Maze's calendar permission in your device's Settings.

Apple Maps

When you type a property, station, stop, rental-car location, or address into a location field, Sky Maze sends search text to Apple's Maps services to show suggestions and check for a matching place. Selecting a suggestion may also use Apple's Maps services to retrieve its location details.

Sky Maze may also check locations after you save travel details or open your trip. Confirmed place names, addresses, coordinates, and available time-zone information are stored with your trip on your device. Time-zone information can help check travel dates and times. These searches can occur before you tap a location button.

When you choose to view a location, Sky Maze passes the relevant location information to Apple Maps. Sky Maze does not request access to your device's GPS location.

The departure and arrival airport buttons open their respective airport locations. When a location action uses a terminal you have entered, Sky Maze may search Apple's Maps services for that airport and terminal. If there is a clear matching terminal, Sky Maze opens its location; otherwise it opens the airport's stored location.

Apple Weather

When you choose View Weather, Sky Maze passes the relevant travel location's coordinates to Apple Weather so you can view its forecast. These are the coordinates of a place associated with your trip, not your device's GPS location. Sky Maze does not download or store weather forecasts inside the app.

Use of Apple's Maps and Weather services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your trip

The trip-sharing screen lets you choose the entire trip or selected events, travelers to include, and whether to include Travel Notes and Confirmations. Sky Maze prepares a trip file locally and opens Apple's share sheet so you can choose a recipient or destination. Travel Notes can include text and note photos; Confirmations can include photos and PDFs. The receiving app or delivery service handles the transfer under its own privacy practices. Sky Maze does not upload this shared trip file to its scanning backend.

Selected screenshots, photos, and PDFs are shared as complete documents. They may contain personal details and information about travelers not selected in the sharing screen. Selecting particular travelers does not redact an attachment or free-form note. Review all included files and notes before sharing.

When a recipient imports a Sky Maze trip file, a copy of its included travel information, travelers, notes, and attachments is saved on their device. The copy is independent: later changes or deletion on the sender's device do not update or revoke the recipient's copy. This is not live collaborative trip synchronization.

If you use a PDF trip-summary export, that summary can include travel details, saved Travel Notes, and note photos. It does not include the separate confirmation attachments. Review the export before sending it. Copies shared or saved outside Sky Maze are controlled by you, the recipient, and the receiving service.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions, eligible free trials, payment processing, and restoring purchases use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles your payment information; Sky Maze does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. The app reads verified transaction and subscription information, including the purchased product, access status, and trial eligibility, to display the appropriate offer and enable subscription features.

Restore Purchases asks Apple to synchronize your purchases and checks for an active Sky Maze subscription; it does not restore locally saved trips or attachments. Subscription checks may also run when the app opens or returns to the foreground. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. Deleting Sky Maze or a trip does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's transaction records.

Your choices and privacy requests

You choose which files to select, submit for scanning, or share. You can change camera and calendar permissions in device Settings, edit your saved content, and delete attachments and previous trips in the app. Files already sent to recipients, calendar accounts, backup services, or processing providers are subject to the separate controls described above.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. To make a request, contact the address below. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant records. Do not send full travel documents or payment details unless necessary. We cannot remotely access your on-device trip library or guarantee deletion of records another service must retain.
