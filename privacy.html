Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This policy describes how Sky Maze and its operator (“we”) handle information in the app and on this website.

Screenshot access and use

Sky Maze reads only the screenshots you choose through the system photo picker to organize your travel details. It does not access your entire photo library or connect to your email account.

Text recognition and parsing happen on your device and may read any visible text, including personal or unrelated information. Screenshots and extracted text are not uploaded to us or an external processing service.

Storage and deletion

Trips are saved on your device until you delete them. They contain travel details such as traveler information, booking codes, providers, properties, routes, addresses, dates, times, seating, vehicle details, and trip progress—not screenshot copies. Selected images and extracted text may remain temporarily in memory or system caches during import and review.

You can edit or delete trips in the app. Moving a trip to Previous trips does not delete it. Deleting the app and its data removes local records; offloading does not. Original screenshots, photo-sync copies, and device backups are managed separately and may remain after trip deletion. We cannot access or delete your device-only records remotely.

Sharing and services

The app has no advertising or behavioral analytics tools. We do not sell personal information or use screenshots or travel details for advertising or AI training.

Choosing View Location sends the lodging address and, for hotel searches, the property name to Apple Maps—not your screenshots or complete trip. Sky Maze does not request your GPS location. Apple Maps operates under its own permissions and privacy policy.

Website privacy

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no advertising cookies, analytics, or cross-site tracking and do not change behavior for Do Not Track signals.

Contact and support information

Email skymaze.support@gmail.com for support, privacy questions, or deletion requests. We receive your email address, messages, and attachments, use them to respond and troubleshoot, and retain them as needed for those purposes and legal obligations. We may verify deletion requests.

Google hosts the support inbox under its Privacy Policy. Unlike importing screenshots into the app, sending them to support transmits them off your device to us and our email provider.

Policy updates

Updates appear here with a revised effective date. We will prominently announce material changes in the app or on this website and obtain consent where required before new uses of information.
