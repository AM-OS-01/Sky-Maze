Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This policy describes how Sky Maze and its operator (“we”) handle information in the app and on this website.

Email access and use

You authorize a supported email account through your provider. Sky Maze receives your email address, available profile name, and authorization credentials—not your password. Access is read-only: the app does not send, change, or delete emails.

When you start a search, Sky Maze examines recent messages to find travel confirmations. This includes processing non-travel messages before you select results. Information accessed includes message identifiers, senders, subjects, dates, contents, and supported PDF or image attachments. Parsing and text recognition happen on your device. Only selected results become saved trips.

Storage and deletion

Trips are stored on your device and may include traveler or guest details, booking codes, providers, properties, routes, full addresses, dates, times, seating, vehicle details, email metadata, and trip progress. We do not operate a server that receives your mailbox or trips.

Search data may remain temporarily in app memory or system caches. Complete emails and attachments are not permanently archived in saved trips. Trips remain until you remove them using the app’s controls or delete the app and its data. Offloading does not erase app data; backups may retain copies according to your settings and must be managed separately.

Disconnecting your email revokes access but does not delete saved trips. Use Disconnect in the app or your provider’s account-permissions settings. Deleting the app alone does not ensure authorization is revoked. Removing trips does not delete original emails. We cannot remotely retrieve, correct, or erase device-only records.

Sharing and services

We use information to organize your chosen trips, provide requested features, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information, use email data for advertising or credit decisions, or train generalized AI models with email contents. The app includes no advertising or behavioral analytics tools. Support staff cannot remotely access your mailbox or trips.

Apple Maps: Choosing View Location sends the full lodging address—and, for hotel searches, the property name—to Apple, not your complete email or timeline. Sky Maze does not request your current GPS location for this feature. Apple Maps may use location according to its own permissions and privacy policies.

Email providers: Your provider processes sign-in and mailbox requests. For Gmail, Google handles authorization and the search covers the last six months, including archived messages but excluding spam and trash. Access can be removed through Google Account connections. Google also hosts our support inbox under its Privacy Policy.

Sky Maze’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We may disclose information we hold when necessary to comply with law or protect security, subject to applicable restrictions on provider data.

Website privacy

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visitors not signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no advertising cookies, analytics, or cross-site tracking and do not change behavior for Do Not Track signals. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

Security

Sky Maze uses HTTPS for email requests, provider authorization tools, and iOS storage protections. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Children

Sky Maze is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if a child has provided personal information directly to us so we can address it and delete information where appropriate.

Policy updates

We will publish updates here with a revised effective date, provide prominent notice of material changes in the app or on the website, and obtain additional consent where required before new uses of information.

Contact and support information

For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning information you send us, email skymaze.support@gmail.com. We receive your email address and any messages or attachments you send through our email services. We use these to respond and troubleshoot, retaining them as needed for those purposes and legal obligations. You may request deletion; we may verify requests and will respond as required by law.
