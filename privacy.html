Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This policy describes how Sky Maze and its operator (“we”) handle information in the app and on this website.

Screenshot access and use

You choose screenshots through the system photo picker. Sky Maze reads the images you select, not your entire photo library. It does not connect to email accounts or request email passwords or authorization credentials.

Text recognition and travel parsing happen on your device. Processing may read any visible text in your selected screenshots, including personal details or unrelated content. Screenshots and extracted text are not uploaded to us or an external processing service. You can review and correct the resulting travel details before saving your trip.

Storage and deletion

Saved trips contain structured travel details, not copies of your screenshots. These details may include traveler or guest information, booking codes, providers, properties, routes, full addresses, dates, times, seating, vehicle details, and trip progress. Trips are stored on your device; we do not operate a server that receives them.

Selected images and extracted text may remain temporarily in app memory or system caches during import and review. Your original screenshots remain in Photos, where you manage them separately. Your photo-sync settings may also retain copies in iCloud or other services.

You can edit saved travel details and delete trips using the app’s controls. Moving a trip to Previous trips does not delete it. Removing trips does not delete your original screenshots or bookings. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records; offloading does not. Backups may retain copies according to your settings and must be managed separately. We cannot remotely retrieve, correct, or erase device-only records.

Sharing and services

We use information to organize your trips, provide requested features, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information or use your screenshots or travel details for advertising or training generalized AI models. The app includes no advertising or behavioral analytics tools. Support staff cannot remotely access your screenshots or saved trips.

Apple Maps: Choosing View Location sends the full lodging address—and, for hotel searches, the property name—to Apple, not your screenshots or complete timeline. Sky Maze does not request your current GPS location for this feature. Apple Maps may use location according to its own permissions and privacy policies.

We may disclose information you send us, such as support messages, when necessary to comply with law or protect security.

Website privacy

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visitors not signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no advertising cookies, analytics, or cross-site tracking and do not change behavior for Do Not Track signals. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

Security

Sky Maze relies on iOS app isolation and storage protections for local trip data. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Children

Sky Maze is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if a child has provided personal information directly to us so we can address it and delete information where appropriate.

Policy updates

We will publish updates here with a revised effective date, provide prominent notice of material changes in the app or on the website, and obtain additional consent where required before new uses of information.

Contact and support information

For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning information you send us, email skymaze.support@gmail.com. We receive your email address and any messages or attachments you send through our email services. We use these to respond and troubleshoot, retaining them as needed for those purposes and legal obligations. You may request deletion; we may verify requests and will respond as required by law.

Google hosts our support inbox under its Privacy Policy. Sending a screenshot to support is separate from importing it into the app: support messages and attachments leave your device.
