Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Your travel details

Sky Maze organizes the travel information you enter. Saved trips and app settings are stored on your device, not uploaded to our servers. No account is required. We do not sell your information or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Deleting trips and backups

You can edit or delete trips in the app. Previous trips remain saved until you delete them. Depending on your settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting a trip does not remove copies in existing backups. We cannot access or delete your device’s trips remotely.

Apple Maps

When you choose View Location or View Airport, the app sends the relevant property name and address or departure-airport details to Apple’s mapping services—not your complete trip. Sky Maze does not request your GPS location. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to its services.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this website and records visitors’ IP addresses for security. We add no tracking cookies or analytics. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Support and privacy questions

Contact skymaze.support@gmail.com. We receive the email address, messages, and attachments you send and keep them only as needed to respond, troubleshoot, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete support correspondence. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or payment information.

Our support inbox is hosted by Google under its Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes and prominently announce material changes in the app or on this website.
