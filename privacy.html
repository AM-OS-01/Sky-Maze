Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

At a glance

  • Your saved trips, travelers, notes, and confirmation attachments are saved on your device. iCloud syncing is enabled by default when available; you can choose On this iPhone in Sky Maze Settings to stop syncing on that device.
  • When you submit files for scanning, images of their contents go through Railway to OpenAI. Visible names, booking codes, and other personal details can be included.
  • Sky Maze uses Aviationstack to refresh eligible flight cards at scheduled times. iOS delivers itinerary reminders separately. Sky Maze does not send alerts for flight changes.
  • Apple and RevenueCat process purchases. Sky Maze’s backend verifies subscription access with Apple and keeps the security records needed to prevent duplicate scan requests.
  • Server backups protect server records. Trip syncing uses iCloud separately and can propagate deletions. Deleted and conflicting content can remain in recovery copies for 30 days; provider records, exported copies, and existing backups have separate retention.

Information stored by Sky Maze

Sky Maze stores your saved travel information, traveler profiles, Travel Notes, photos and PDF confirmations, and app settings locally on your device. This includes the independent Travel Confirmations collection on the starter screen. Travel Notes stay with their events and confirmations stay with their trip when it moves to Previous Trips. Temporary files and unfinished work are handled separately as described below. A separate Sky Maze account is not required. Sky Maze does not sell your information or use advertising trackers. Purchase management involves RevenueCat as described below.

Local storage does not mean every feature works only on your device. When you submit files for scanning, their contents are processed by Sky Maze's backend and OpenAI. Device verification, subscriptions and access checks, location searches, and sharing also involve the services described below. When iCloud is available and the iCloud storage option is selected, saved trips and the other information listed below sync through Apple CloudKit; they are not stored in the scanning backend’s database.

Private iCloud syncing

iCloud is selected by default. When you are signed into iCloud and iCloud access is available for Sky Maze, the app automatically syncs committed saved information through Apple CloudKit’s private database for that Apple Account. This includes active and previous trips, travelers and their booking details, completed events, Travel Notes, saved note photos, confirmation photos and original PDFs with their file names, and the separate starter-screen confirmation collection. Previously saved packing lists, expenses, Sun places, and currency, time-zone, and plug preferences continue to sync. The app does not send this trip library to RevenueCat or the scanning backend for syncing.

You can choose On this iPhone in Sky Maze Settings to keep using saved content locally and stop trip-library syncing on that device. The app finishes downloading available content before completing this switch; a failed switch keeps the previous choice. This does not delete the existing iCloud copy or change the choice on other devices. Switching back to iCloud combines local and cloud changes, including edits and deletions made while syncing was off, using the conflict and recovery behavior described below. The iCloud option also retains local copies for offline use. The Storage estimate counts saved PDFs, photos and note text; it is not your total device storage or iCloud plan allowance.

Saved travel details and PDFs can contain names, booking references, locations, dates, embedded document metadata, and information about other travelers. Apple processes and stores the synced content under its Privacy Policy and your iCloud settings. A separate Sky Maze login is not required. Sync uses your Apple Account’s available iCloud storage. Local saving remains available offline; changes wait for connectivity and iCloud availability. Delivery can be delayed and is not guaranteed to be immediate.

Unfinished scan and review drafts, incoming share-menu queues, device permissions, Calendar links, notification identifiers, sensor readings, and device caches are not included in this trip sync. Purchase access uses the separate services described below. On compatible versions using the same Apple Account, the saved library and one-active-trip selection can synchronize across devices; this does not make a shared trip sent to another person a live collaborative document.

Changes and deletions propagate between devices. Sky Maze keeps recovery copies of deleted content and conflicting edits for 30 days, including the attachments needed to recover a trip. Settings offers recovery for supported saved items. Expired recovery content is removed during later cleanup and syncing; an offline device, provider retention, or an existing backup can retain copies longer. Minimal deletion and sync-history records may remain to prevent old devices from bringing deleted items back. Syncing is not an independent backup.

If the Apple Account changes, Sky Maze pauses syncing and keeps local information instead of uploading the previous account’s data to a different account. Returning to the original account resumes syncing. Removing cloud data outside Sky Maze can also pause sync and require an explicit restore of the retained local copy. You can manage Sky Maze’s iCloud access, stored iCloud data, and backups in Apple’s settings. Turning off access or uninstalling the app does not itself erase existing cloud copies.

Sky Maze uses Apple’s background push service for silent sync updates. These updates are separate from the local trip reminders described below. Sync status, manual syncing, and available recovery copies appear in the app’s Settings.

Scanning travel confirmations

When you submit selected files for scanning, Sky Maze prepares images on your device and sends them over HTTPS to its backend, hosted on Railway, which passes them to the OpenAI API to extract travel information. PDF pages are rendered as images for scanning; the original PDF and its file name are not sent through this scanning request. Image preparation removes embedded image metadata, but it does not redact information visible on the page.

The service can therefore receive any information visible in the files you submit, including names, contact information, travel dates, locations, reservation references, and information about other travelers. Only submit files you have permission to use, and remove unnecessary sensitive information before scanning. Selecting a file, sharing it into Sky Maze, or saving it to Travel Confirmations does not start a scan. Scanning begins when you submit the selected files in the app.

Extracted information is returned to the app for your review and correction before you save it. When you add to an existing trip, Sky Maze may suggest updating details in an existing travel event or adding a new event. Confirmations saved with the trip are available in its Travel Confirmations. Original PDFs are kept as complete documents. Review the extracted details, suggested changes, and attached files before saving.

The current backend processes scan images and extracted results in memory and does not write that content to its application database or application logs. Temporary drafts, prepared images, and copies of original confirmations may be written to your device during upload, scanning, and review. Unfinished work can remain available while the same app session is running, including when you switch away and return without the app restarting.

On a fresh app launch, Sky Maze discards unfinished trip setup, scan results, and review state instead of restoring or automatically resubmitting them. This applies whether you force-close the app or iOS ends its process. Saved trips, notes, booking attachments, and the starter-screen confirmation collection are not discarded. Saving or discarding a draft, or cleanup on a fresh launch, removes unneeded staging files; an interruption, unreadable file, or failed cleanup can leave temporary copies on the device. Closing the app or canceling a scan cannot guarantee that processing already started by the backend or OpenAI stops or that provider-held records are deleted.

Sky Maze sends scan requests with OpenAI response storage disabled. This does not guarantee zero retention by OpenAI. Under OpenAI's published API policy, API data is not used for model training by default unless the API customer opts in; abuse-monitoring content may be retained for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Other temporary processing storage may apply. See OpenAI's API data controls. Deleting a local trip does not erase provider records already retained under those policies.

Device verification and service records

Sky Maze uses Apple's App Attest to help verify app installations and protect the scanning service from unauthorized use. Verification may run when the app opens, before you submit any files. It does not send screenshots or trip contents. The backend stores an app-installation key identifier, public verification key, attestation receipt, creation time, and anti-replay counter. These are security identifiers, not advertising identifiers.

The backend verifies an Apple-signed app transaction and derives a hashed, environment-specific account identifier. The app uses that identifier with RevenueCat to link purchases across devices. The backend stores scan request identifiers, account identifiers, and scan states to prevent duplicate processing. These records do not contain travel files or scan results. Hashing an account identifier does not make the records anonymous. Historical credit records, if any, remain separate.

An unfinished scan reservation expires after five minutes. The request record may remain to prevent a duplicate scan. Verification challenges expire after five minutes and are cleaned up after ten minutes when another challenge is created. Separate legacy or private operator-test records may include a fingerprint of submitted image content, the request day, and a pseudonymous installation identifier.

Application error logs can include a request identifier but not uploaded images or extracted results. Hosting and network providers may also process connection information, such as IP addresses and request timing, to deliver and secure the service. Their operational records and backups have separate retention practices. Deleting the app or a trip does not automatically remove server-side security records. Contact us about those records or other privacy requests.

Backups and deletion

You can edit saved travel details and Travel Notes and delete individual attachments in the app. Remove Trip moves the active trip to Previous Trips; it does not delete the saved information. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips removes it from the active local library and queues the deletion for iCloud syncing. Its associated Travel Notes, note photos, and confirmations can remain in recovery copies as described below. Removing an imported attachment does not delete the original from Photos or Files.

The starter-screen Travel Confirmations collection is separate from your trips. Its files remain until you delete them from that collection, even if a trip using a copy is deleted. Deleting a file from the collection does not delete copies already saved with trips. Location-to-time-zone cache entries can also remain on the device after the associated trip is deleted. Those entries have no automatic expiration. Uninstalling the app removes its ordinary local app data, but does not delete its private iCloud copy, server-side records, external copies, or backups.

Copies waiting to be imported from the share menu are stored separately. If an import cannot be completed, those copies may remain on your device even if you delete the intended trip. See Sharing into Sky Maze below. Deleting content in Sky Maze also does not delete copies you previously exported to another app or recipient.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting a trip in Sky Maze does not remove copies from existing backups. The scanning backend cannot restore your trip library or remotely delete your device backups. Manage iCloud data and device backups through Apple’s settings and controls.

Sky Maze uses automated daily Railway backups of its persistent server records. These records can include device verification, scan coordination, flight schedules, and cached flight results. The backups do not contain your on-device trip library, and the scanning backend does not save submitted images or extracted travel results to that storage. RevenueCat separately maintains purchase records. Server backups cannot restore trips or attachments lost from your phone.

Removing a server record from the active service does not immediately remove copies in existing backups. Backup copies may remain until they expire or are removed under the relevant backup schedule. Provider-held purchase, processing, and operational records are subject to the providers' retention practices described in this policy.

Note photos

When you tap the camera in a Travel Note, Sky Maze requests camera access so you can take a photo for that note. You can also select an existing photo using Apple's photo picker; Sky Maze receives only the photos you select, without requesting access to your entire photo library. Photos saved to a note are stored locally as resized copies without the original photo's embedded metadata. Copies waiting to be imported from another app may still contain that metadata, as explained below. Captured photos are not automatically saved to your photo library. Saved note photos are included in private iCloud syncing when available and the iCloud storage option is selected. Importing or deleting a note attachment does not change the original photo in your library. Previously attached photos remain with their notes. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips also removes its note attachments from the app.

Confirmations

You can select PDFs using Apple’s Files picker or share them from another app, such as your email app. You can save confirmations with a trip or keep them in the starter-screen collection for later. Sky Maze receives the files you choose. If a file is stored in iCloud Drive or with another file provider, that provider may download it to your device under its own privacy policy. Sky Maze does not access your email account or automatically update original documents when trip details change.

Sky Maze saves local copies of confirmations. It keeps original PDFs, which can contain personal information and embedded metadata, and stores thumbnails, file names, and page counts. Previously saved confirmation photos may also remain in your library. Files submitted for scanning are processed as described above.

Confirmations saved with a trip stay with it in Previous Trips until you remove the file or delete the trip. The PDF viewer may offer Apple's sharing controls, which send the selected document to the app or destination you choose. Review the document before sharing it.

Sharing into Sky Maze

When you choose Sky Maze in another app’s share menu, you can share selected PDFs to start an upload for a new trip, save them to a trip’s Travel Confirmations, or save them for later in the starter-screen collection. You can also share supported text and photos to a Travel Note for a selected event in your active trip. Imported notes use plain text. The copies do not stay linked to the source app.

The app and its share extension use a shared storage area on your device. It holds summaries of available trips and events, incoming items, and the identifiers needed to route them to the destination you choose. The source app does not gain access to your trip library.

Incoming copies may contain original photo or PDF metadata. Copies are queued on your device until the app processes them. A failed import or unavailable destination can leave queued files on the device for recovery. Sharing into Sky Maze does not upload the files to the backend. If you later submit them for scanning, the scanning process described above applies.

Sharing a Travel Note

The share button inside a Travel Note opens Apple's share sheet with that note's text and attached note photos. It does not include the booking's separate confirmation files. Sky Maze prepares the content locally and removes its temporary export files when sharing finishes or is dismissed. You choose the receiving app or destination, such as Apple Notes, which may store or sync the received copy according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Sharing a note does not create ongoing synchronization. Editing or deleting the note in Sky Maze does not change copies already shared elsewhere. Review the text and photos before sharing, especially if they contain booking codes or other personal information.

Calendar

When you choose Add to Calendar, Sky Maze requests full calendar access through Apple's permission prompt so it can add your trip's events and find and update the calendar events it previously created. It stores the linked event identifiers and synchronization details locally on your device. It does not use unrelated calendar events for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Calendar events can include travel dates and times, routes, locations, company and service details, terminal, gate, seat, and booking confirmation codes when provided. Your Travel Note text, note photos, and attached confirmation files are not added to Calendar. Changes to travel details can update previously linked calendar events.

Events are saved to your default writable calendar and may sync through the account associated with that calendar, such as iCloud or another provider. That provider's privacy policy applies. Moving a trip to Previous Trips or deleting it from Sky Maze does not remove copies already saved to Calendar. You can manage those events in Calendar and change Sky Maze's calendar permission in your device's Settings.

Trip notifications

Trip reminders are on by default for flights, hotels, vacation rentals, trains, and coach buses. They require your iOS notification permission, and you can turn off any category in Sky Maze’s notification settings. The app schedules reminders using your active trip. iOS stores the reminder content and scheduled times on your device for delivery.

Reminders can display travel times, routes, airports and stations, transport companies and service numbers, property names, and connection details. This information may appear on your Lock Screen depending on your iPhone’s notification-preview settings.

When Sky Maze processes edits, receives flight details, or moves on from a trip event, it can reschedule or remove pending reminders. Sky Maze does not send separate notifications for flight delays, cancellations, or gate or terminal changes. Flight details from scheduled checks appear in the app.

Flight updates

When flight updates are available, Sky Maze sends flight numbers, departure and arrival airport codes, the local scheduled departure date, and available scheduled departure and arrival times for eligible flights to its backend on Railway. This can happen when the app opens, resumes, or syncs a changed trip. The backend uses a device verification identifier and the verified subscription end time to maintain the check schedule.

Railway schedules checks with Aviationstack about one hour before scheduled departure and, when a scheduled arrival time is available, four hours after scheduled arrival. Checks can run while the app is closed. A flight can still qualify for the later check after it is marked complete or moved to Previous Trips. Requests to Aviationstack include the flight number and airport codes, plus the departure date for historical lookups. They do not include traveler names, reservation codes, seat assignments, confirmation files, or Sky Maze device or account identifiers.

Returned details such as flight times, gates, terminals, and status may update the flight card. Confirmed flight outcomes may be saved with the trip, including in Previous Trips, and appear in later PDF summary exports. The backend removes expired flight schedules during cleanup. Cached flight results older than 14 days are removed during later checks. Existing backups and provider records follow their own retention practices. Aviationstack is operated by APILayer. See Idera’s Privacy Policy.

Apple Maps

When you type a property, station, stop, or address into a location field, Sky Maze sends search text to Apple's Maps services to show suggestions and check for a matching place. Selecting a suggestion may also use Apple's Maps services to retrieve its location details.

Sky Maze may also check locations after you save travel details or open your trip. Confirmed place names, addresses, coordinates, and available time-zone information are stored with your trip on your device. To schedule reminders and determine the relevant booking card, Sky Maze may send a booking address or its coordinates to Apple's geocoding services to resolve its time zone. A temporary lookup failure may be retried once. These searches can occur before you tap a location button; they do not use your device's GPS location.

Successful time-zone lookups are cached locally, keyed by the booking address or coordinates, so they can be reused without another lookup, including when offline. Unknown time zones are not guessed for reminder scheduling. Cached location/time-zone entries are separate from the saved trip and have no automatic expiration.

The trip map uses Apple Maps to show a selected place and load Look Around imagery on request. When you choose to center the map on your location or get driving or walking directions, Sky Maze can request location access while using the app. With permission, the map can show your current location and heading. Apple Maps uses your location and the selected stop for routes and travel-time estimates. Choosing Transit opens the selected stop in Apple Maps for transit directions. Sky Maze keeps your live location out of saved trips and its backend.

The departure and arrival airport buttons open their respective airport locations. When a location action uses a terminal you have entered, Sky Maze may search Apple's Maps services for that airport and terminal. If there is a clear matching terminal, Sky Maze opens its location; otherwise it opens the airport's stored location.

Apple Weather

With an active subscription, Sky Maze can request a forecast from Apple Weather when an eligible travel card is visible, even before you choose View Weather. The request uses coordinates for the trip location. Sky Maze keeps a temporary forecast and location cache on your device. That cache is separate from your trip library and does not sync through iCloud.

Use of Apple's Maps and Weather services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your trip

The trip-sharing screen lets you choose the entire trip or selected events, travelers to include, and whether to include Travel Notes and Confirmations. Sky Maze prepares a trip file locally and opens Apple's share sheet so you can choose a recipient or destination. Travel Notes can include text and note photos; Confirmations can include photos and PDFs. The receiving app or delivery service handles the transfer under its own privacy practices. Sky Maze does not upload this shared trip file to its scanning backend.

Selected screenshots, photos, and PDFs are shared as complete documents. They may contain personal details and information about travelers not selected in the sharing screen. Selecting particular travelers does not redact an attachment or free-form note. Review all included files and notes before sharing.

When a recipient imports a Sky Maze trip file, a copy of its included travel information, travelers, notes, and attachments is saved on their device. The copy is independent: later changes or deletion on the sender's device do not update or revoke the recipient's copy. This is not live collaborative trip synchronization.

If you use a PDF trip-summary export, that summary can include travel details, saved Travel Notes, and note photos. It does not include the separate confirmation attachments. Review the export before sending it. Copies shared or saved outside Sky Maze are controlled by you, the recipient, and the receiving service.

Subscriptions and purchases

Sky Maze offers a monthly auto-renewing subscription. Purchases use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit, with RevenueCat managing checkout and purchase records. Apple handles payment information. Sky Maze does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password.

RevenueCat receives app user identifiers, including the hashed, environment-specific Apple app-account identifier, along with App Store purchase information and app or device information needed to validate purchases, restore access, and provide purchase reporting. Sky Maze does not send your travel confirmations, trip details, notes, or photos to RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes purchase data under its privacy policy.

For subscription checks and scans, the app sends an Apple-signed app transaction proof that identifies this app’s relationship with your Apple Account. The backend verifies that proof and checks the subscription with Apple. It does not retain signed proofs or send them to OpenAI. Verification may run when the app opens or comes to the foreground, when you open subscription settings, before scanning, or after a purchase or restore.

You can manage or cancel renewal through Apple’s subscription settings. Canceling leaves access available through the current paid period. Once access ends, new scans and forecast updates require an active subscription. Your saved trips and local attachments remain available. Deleting the app does not cancel renewal or automatically delete purchase records held by Sky Maze, Apple, or RevenueCat.

Your choices and privacy requests

You choose which files to select, submit for scanning, or share. You can change camera, calendar, location, and notification permissions in device Settings. In Sky Maze, you can change your trip storage option, edit your saved content, and delete confirmations, attachments, and previous trips. Files already sent to recipients, calendar accounts, backup services, or processing providers are subject to the separate controls described above.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. To make a request, contact the address below. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant records. Do not send full travel documents or payment details unless necessary. We cannot remotely access your on-device trip library or guarantee deletion of records another service must retain.
