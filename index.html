Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Information stored by Sky Maze

Sky Maze stores the travel information and app settings you enter locally on your device. No account is required. Sky Maze does not sell your information or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Backups and deletion

You can edit or delete saved trips in the app. Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups. Sky Maze cannot access or remotely delete information stored on your device or in your backups.

Apple Maps

When you type a property, station, stop, rental-car location, or address into a location field, Sky Maze sends search text to Apple's Maps services to show suggestions and check for a matching place. Selecting a suggestion also uses Apple's Maps services to retrieve its location details.

Sky Maze may also check locations after you save travel details or open your trip. Confirmed place names, addresses, and coordinates are stored with your trip on your device. These searches can occur before you tap a location button.

When you choose to view a location, Sky Maze passes the relevant location information to Apple Maps. Sky Maze does not request access to your device's GPS location.

Apple Weather

When you choose View Weather, Sky Maze passes the relevant destination coordinates to Apple Weather so you can view its forecast. Sky Maze does not download or store weather forecasts inside the app.

Use of Apple's Maps and Weather services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Sky Maze does not add analytics or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub may process information as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy questions, email skymaze.support@gmail.com. Information you send by email is used only as needed to respond to your request or meet legal obligations.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Sky Maze changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
