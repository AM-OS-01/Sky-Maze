Sky Maze is an iPhone app that brings the travel confirmations you choose into one trip timeline.

From your inbox to your itinerary

Connect a supported email account, search for travel confirmations, and choose which results to add. Organize flights, hotels, vacation rentals, trains, coach buses, and rental cars. Keep previous trips together and open supported lodging locations in Apple Maps.

Sky Maze reads recent email on your device to find confirmations, then saves only the results you choose. Read how access, storage, and sharing work in the Privacy Policy.

Sky Maze can make mistakes. Check important details against your original booking confirmations.

Have a question? Contact Sky Maze support.
