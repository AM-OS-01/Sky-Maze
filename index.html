Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Information stored by Sky Maze

Sky Maze stores your travel information, unfinished form drafts, Travel Notes, attached photos and PDF confirmations, and app settings locally on your device. Notes and confirmations are saved with their respective bookings and remain with the trip when it moves to Previous Trips. No account is required. Sky Maze does not operate a server that receives your saved trip content, sell your information, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Some features use Apple services or let you send information to other apps. Location searches may also run automatically after you save travel details or open a trip. These uses, and the local storage used when importing shared items, are explained below.

Backups and deletion

You can edit saved travel details and Travel Notes and delete individual attachments in the app. Remove Trip moves the active trip to Previous Trips; it does not delete the saved information. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips removes that trip and its associated Travel Notes, note photos, and confirmations from the app's local storage. Removing an imported attachment does not delete the original from Photos or Files.

Copies waiting to be imported from the share menu are stored separately. If an import cannot be completed, those copies may remain on your device even if you delete the intended trip. See Sharing into Sky Maze below. Deleting content in Sky Maze also does not delete copies you previously exported to another app or recipient.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting a trip in Sky Maze does not remove copies from existing backups. Sky Maze cannot access or remotely delete information stored on your device or in your backups.

Note photos

When you tap the camera in a Travel Note, Sky Maze requests camera access so you can take a photo for that note. You can also select an existing photo using Apple's photo picker; Sky Maze receives only the photos you select, without requesting access to your entire photo library. Photos saved to a note are stored locally as resized copies without the original photo's embedded metadata. Copies waiting to be imported from another app may still contain that metadata, as explained below. Captured photos are not automatically saved to your photo library or uploaded by Sky Maze. Importing or deleting a note attachment does not change the original photo in your library. Previously attached photos remain with their notes. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips also removes its note attachments from the app.

Confirmations

You can attach selected photos and PDF files to a booking using Apple's Photos and Files pickers. Sky Maze receives the items you choose, not unrestricted access to your photo library or files. If you choose a file stored with iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may download it to your device under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sky Maze saves local copies of these attachments. Photos saved to a booking are resized and re-encoded without the original photo's embedded metadata; copies waiting to be imported from another app may still contain that metadata. PDFs are copied in their original form and may retain embedded metadata and personal information. Sky Maze also stores thumbnails and, for PDFs, the file name and page count. It does not access your email account, extract booking details from these attachments, or automatically update them when a booking changes.

Attachments stay with the booking in Previous Trips until you delete the attachment or the saved trip. The PDF viewer may offer Apple's sharing controls; using them sends the selected document to the app or destination you choose. Review the document before sharing it.

Sharing into Sky Maze

When you choose Sky Maze in another app's share menu, Sky Maze receives the supported items that app passes with your action, such as selected PDFs, photos, or note text. You choose a travel event in your active trip as the destination. An active trip is required; Sky Maze does not offer a separate save-for-later collection.

Shared PDFs and photos can be saved as confirmations. Shared note text, including text sent as a copy from Apple Notes, is appended to the selected event's Travel Note, along with supported note photos. Text is imported as plain text, so formatting may differ from the original. This is a copy, not a connection to your Notes library or email account. Later changes in either app do not automatically update the other copy.

The app and its share extension use a shared storage area on your device. It holds a summary of available travel events so you can choose a destination, along with incoming items and the identifiers needed to attach them to the correct event. This does not give the source app unrestricted access to your saved trips.

Incoming copies are queued locally until Sky Maze saves them to the selected event. The queued copy is removed after a successful import. If an import fails or its destination is no longer available, the queued copy may remain on your device for recovery; there is no automatic expiration. These incoming copies can contain original photo or PDF metadata. They are not uploaded to a Sky Maze server.

Sharing a Travel Note

The share button inside a Travel Note opens Apple's share sheet with that note's text and attached note photos. It does not include the booking's separate confirmation files. Sky Maze prepares the content locally and removes its temporary export files when sharing finishes or is dismissed. You choose the receiving app or destination, such as Apple Notes, which may store or sync the received copy according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Sharing a note does not create ongoing synchronization. Editing or deleting the note in Sky Maze does not change copies already shared elsewhere. Review the text and photos before sharing, especially if they contain booking codes or other personal information.

Calendar

When you choose Add to Calendar, Sky Maze requests full calendar access through Apple's permission prompt so it can add your trip's events and find and update the calendar events it previously created. It stores the linked event identifiers and synchronization details locally on your device. It does not use unrelated calendar events for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Calendar events can include travel dates and times, routes, locations, company and service details, terminal, gate, seat, and booking confirmation codes when provided. Your Travel Note text, note photos, and attached confirmation files are not added to Calendar. Changes to travel details can update previously linked calendar events.

Events are saved to your default writable calendar and may sync through the account associated with that calendar, such as iCloud or another provider. That provider's privacy policy applies. Moving a trip to Previous Trips or deleting it from Sky Maze does not remove copies already saved to Calendar. You can manage those events in Calendar and change Sky Maze's calendar permission in your device's Settings.

Apple Maps

When you type a property, station, stop, rental-car location, or address into a location field, Sky Maze sends search text to Apple's Maps services to show suggestions and check for a matching place. Selecting a suggestion may also use Apple's Maps services to retrieve its location details.

Sky Maze may also check locations after you save travel details or open your trip. Confirmed place names, addresses, coordinates, and available time-zone information are stored with your trip on your device. Time-zone information can help check travel dates and times. These searches can occur before you tap a location button.

When you choose to view a location, Sky Maze passes the relevant location information to Apple Maps. Sky Maze does not request access to your device's GPS location.

The departure and arrival airport buttons open their respective airport locations. When a location action uses a terminal you have entered, Sky Maze may search Apple's Maps services for that airport and terminal. If there is a clear matching terminal, Sky Maze opens its location; otherwise it opens the airport's stored location.

Apple Weather

When you choose View Weather, Sky Maze passes the relevant travel location's coordinates to Apple Weather so you can view its forecast. These are the coordinates of a place associated with your trip, not your device's GPS location. Sky Maze does not download or store weather forecasts inside the app.

Use of Apple's Maps and Weather services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your trip

When you tap Share, Sky Maze creates a PDF summary on your device and opens Apple's share sheet. The PDF includes the selected current or previous trip's travel details, such as routes, dates, times, saved addresses, and terminal, gate, or seat information, along with each travel event's saved Travel Note and note photos. Confirmation-code fields and files attached through Confirmations are not included, but anything you write or photograph in a Travel Note is included. Review your notes and photos before sharing. Sky Maze does not upload the PDF to its own server. You choose whether and where to share or save it. Copies you share or save outside Sky Maze are controlled by you and the receiving app or recipient; changing or deleting your trip does not update or delete those copies.
