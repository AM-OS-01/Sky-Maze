Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Information stored by Sky Maze

Sky Maze stores the travel information, Travel Notes, and app settings you enter locally on your device. Travel Notes are saved with their respective travel cards and remain with the trip when it moves to Previous Trips. No account is required. Sky Maze does not sell your information or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Backups and deletion

You can edit saved travel details and Travel Notes in the app. Remove Trip moves the active trip to Previous Trips; it does not delete the saved information. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips removes that trip and its associated Travel Notes from the app's local storage.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting a trip in Sky Maze does not remove copies from existing backups. Sky Maze cannot access or remotely delete information stored on your device or in your backups.

Note photos

When you tap the camera in a Travel Note, Sky Maze requests camera access so you can take a photo for that note. You can also select an existing photo using Apple's photo picker; Sky Maze receives only the photos you select, without requesting access to your entire photo library. Photos are stored locally as resized copies without the original photo's embedded metadata. Captured photos are not automatically saved to your photo library or uploaded by Sky Maze. Importing or deleting a note attachment does not change the original photo in your library. Previously attached photos remain with their notes. Deleting a trip from Previous Trips also removes its note attachments from the app.

Apple Maps

When you type a property, station, stop, rental-car location, or address into a location field, Sky Maze sends search text to Apple's Maps services to show suggestions and check for a matching place. Selecting a suggestion also uses Apple's Maps services to retrieve its location details.

Sky Maze may also check locations after you save travel details or open your trip. Confirmed place names, addresses, and coordinates are stored with your trip on your device. These searches can occur before you tap a location button.

When you choose to view a location, Sky Maze passes the relevant location information to Apple Maps. Sky Maze does not request access to your device's GPS location.

When you tap Go to Airport and have entered a terminal, Sky Maze searches Apple's Maps services for that airport and terminal. If there is a clear matching terminal, Sky Maze opens its location; otherwise it opens the airport's stored location.

Apple Weather

When you choose View Weather, Sky Maze passes the relevant travel location's coordinates to Apple Weather so you can view its forecast. These are the coordinates of a place associated with your trip, not your device's GPS location. Sky Maze does not download or store weather forecasts inside the app.

Use of Apple's Maps and Weather services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your trip

When you tap Share, Sky Maze creates a PDF summary on your device and opens Apple's share sheet. The PDF includes the selected current or previous trip's travel details, such as routes, dates, times, saved addresses, and terminal, gate, or seat information, along with each travel event's saved Travel Note and attached photos. Confirmation-code fields are not included, but anything you write or photograph in a Travel Note is included. Review your notes and photos before sharing. Sky Maze does not upload the PDF to its own server. You choose whether and where to share or save it. Copies you share or save outside Sky Maze are controlled by you and the receiving app or recipient; changing or deleting your trip does not update or delete those copies.
