Sky Maze is an iPhone app that turns screenshots of your travel confirmations into one trip timeline.

From screenshots to your itinerary

Choose screenshots, review and correct the travel details, then save your trip. Organize flights, hotels, vacation rentals, trains, coach buses, and rental cars. Keep previous trips together and open supported lodging locations in Apple Maps.

Text recognition and parsing happen on your device. Sky Maze does not connect to your email account or upload your screenshots for processing. Read about storage and sharing in the Privacy Policy.

Sky Maze can make mistakes. Check important details against your original booking confirmations.

Have a question? Contact Sky Maze support.
