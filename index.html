Sky Maze is an iPhone app that keeps your travel details together in one trip timeline.

Your trip, in one place

Choose a travel category, enter its details, and save. Review your cards, then continue to your trip. Organize flights, hotels, vacation rentals, trains, coach buses, and rental cars. Keep previous trips together and open supported lodging locations in Apple Maps.

You enter and edit the details yourself. The current trip flow does not scan screenshots, use AI extraction, or connect to your email account. Read about storage and sharing in the Privacy Policy.

Check important details against your original booking confirmations.

Have a question? Contact Sky Maze support.
