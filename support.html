How can we help?

For app support or privacy questions, email skymaze.support@gmail.com.

Reporting an issue

Describe the issue and include your app and iOS versions. If you send a screenshot, hide personal details and booking codes. Never send passwords or payment details.

For booking changes or cancellations, contact your travel provider. For email access, saved trips, and deletion information, see the Privacy Policy.
