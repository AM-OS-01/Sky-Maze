How can we help?

For app support or privacy questions, email skymaze.support@gmail.com.

Reporting an issue

Describe the issue and include your app and iOS versions. For an entry or display issue, explain what you entered and what you expected to see. If you send a screenshot to support, hide personal details and booking codes. Never send passwords or payment details.

You can edit your entered travel details in the app. For changes or cancellations to the actual booking, contact your travel provider. For saved trips and deletion information, see the Privacy Policy.
